Why it matters now

Fencing is now a private equity trade. Here's what that means for your company.

The U.S. fencing market is worth roughly $9.4–9.8B and is on track for $14.9B by 2033. More than 50,000 professional installers split it, and the ten biggest hold about 4%.

That fragmentation is exactly what roll-up buyers look for. Sponsors buy founder-run fence companies at 4.0–6.5× EBITDA, centralize the back office and marketing, and build regional platforms that trade at 10–14×. Superior Fence & Rail grew from $66M to more than $300M in revenue on standardized digital lead pipelines. Those platforms are now buying ads in your ZIP codes.

Most of the gap between a company that gets squeezed and one that grows, or sells at a premium, is measurable: reviews, Map Pack position, website conversion, how fast you answer, and whether your site lets a homeowner price a fence before you drive out. We measure all of it.

A new puppy or a new pool turns a fence into a deadline. Pool codes dictate height, self-closing hinges, and anti-climb layouts, and a storm-downed section can't wait. These buyers pick the company that looks credible and answers first.

  • A top-3 Map Pack position for "fence repair" and "pool fence" in every town you serve
  • Pages that spell out pool-code and pet-containment specs: heights, latches, ground clearance
  • Tap-to-call, instant text-back, and an install-timeline promise above the fold
  • Recent reviews and job photos that read as "local and legit"

Upgrade buyers research materials, compare bids, and stall on property lines, permits, and HOA rules. Contractors who handle the survey pins, the permit, and the HOA packet win the job, and an online quoting tool filters out tire-kickers before anyone drives out.

  • An interactive estimator where homeowners trace their fence line and see a price range
  • Material pages (vinyl privacy, horizontal cedar, ornamental aluminum) that rank and convert
  • Clear answers on boundary surveys, permits, HOA approval, and 811 utility locates
  • Proof of in-house, insured crews and a workmanship warranty, separate from the manufacturer's
And the high-margin lane: commercial and gates. North American perimeter security is a $24B market. Automated gates, access control, and recurring maintenance contracts reward AFA-credentialed (CGAD/CGAT, UL 325, ASTM F2200) contractors. We track who's winning property managers in your market, too.
